## Evidence

- The best periodic model has a period of 773.3 days and covers 8.6 cycles in 936 points over 6650 days; its fundamental has an amplitude of 0.239 in magnitudes, and 10% of its power is in higher harmonics.
- Against red noise fitted to this object, with the search over 817 trial periods included, the probability of a periodic model this good is 0.09 (10 of 111 simulated nulls matched it).
- For contrast, a test that assumes white noise would have given below 1 in a billion (7.4e-36); that number is not evidence, because the noise is not white.
- The period of 773.3 days is 9.6 resolution elements from the nearest calendar period (one year); that is clear of it.
- The observing pattern has 2% of its power at this period; the sampling does not explain the signal.
- Removing any one of 19 years moves the phase by at most 0.01 cycles and the amplitude by a factor of 0.92 to 1.07; the most important year carries 20% of the evidence. The signal does not hang on one year.
- The signal is visible in fewer than two bands: no comparison possible.
- Searched alone, the first 70% of the time span prefers a period of 48.2 days, not 773.3: the early data do not find this signal independently.
- The warped-time test was not run.
